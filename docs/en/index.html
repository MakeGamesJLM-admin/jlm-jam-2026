What happens at a game jam?

Like a hackathon, but for games!

We kick off on Tuesday, the fifth night of Hanukkah, by revealing the secret theme. Then we brainstorm, form teams, and have 48 hours to make a game from scratch. At the end, we’ll show off our games and play them together!

Your first game jam?

With the tools available today, it’s easier than ever to get started. The community is here to help when you get stuck, and mentors will be available to support teams during the jam.

Things to know

Who can participate?

Programmers, designers, writers, musicians, and anyone curious about making games. Beginners and experienced creators are all welcome!

Coming without a team?

We’ll set aside time at the beginning to meet people and form teams. You can also bring your own team.

What should I bring?

A laptop, charger, and any equipment you want to work with. We recommend installing and updating your software in advance.

Do I need to stay for all 48 hours?

You can take breaks and go home to rest. We recommend being there for the kickoff and final game presentations.

Can I sleep at Hansen House?

Yes! There’ll be a dedicated darkened area with beanbags. We recommend bringing a sleeping bag.
